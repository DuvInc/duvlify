<Steps>
  <Step title="A content importer">
    This will fetch a published site, rewrite its MDX, re-encode its images,
    and emit `content/` plus a starting `navigation` block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A redirect map">
    This map will be generated from the old URL structure rather than
    assembled by hand, because a hand-written redirect map is where a
    migration silently loses pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A verification pass">
    This pass will assert that every page in the old sitemap resolves in the
    new build, so the migration is checked rather than spot-read.
  </Step>
</Steps>
